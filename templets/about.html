Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about CarHub, our services, and the car buying process.

What types of vehicles do you offer?
+

CarHub specializes in premium and luxury vehicles, including supercars, sports cars, classic automobiles, and high-end SUVs. Our collection features brands like Lamborghini, Ferrari, Porsche, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Aston Martin, McLaren, and many more. We carefully curate our inventory to ensure we offer only the finest vehicles that meet our strict quality standards.

Do you offer financing options?
+

Yes, we provide comprehensive financing solutions tailored to your needs. We work with leading financial institutions to offer competitive rates and flexible payment terms. Our finance specialists will help you find the best financing option that fits your budget and preferences, whether you're looking for traditional auto loans, lease options, or specialized luxury vehicle financing.

What is your return and warranty policy?
+

We offer a comprehensive 7-day return policy for your peace of mind. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return the vehicle within 7 days of delivery. All our vehicles come with detailed inspection reports and any applicable manufacturer warranties. We also offer extended warranty options and service packages to protect your investment long-term.

Can I schedule a test drive?
+

Absolutely! We encourage test drives for all our vehicles. You can schedule a test drive by contacting us directly or using our online booking system. Our experienced team will coordinate a convenient time for you to experience the vehicle firsthand. For high-value vehicles, we may require pre-qualification and valid insurance verification.

Do you provide vehicle history reports?
+

Yes, transparency is fundamental to our business. We provide comprehensive vehicle history reports for all our cars, including detailed maintenance records, accident history (if any), ownership history, and any modifications. Our multi-point inspection process ensures that every vehicle meets our high standards before being offered for sale.

Do you accept trade-ins?
+

Yes, we accept trade-ins on qualifying vehicles. Our appraisal team will assess your current vehicle and provide a fair market valuation that can be applied toward your new purchase. We handle all the paperwork and make the trade-in process as seamless as possible. Contact us to schedule a trade-in evaluation.

What about delivery and shipping?
+

We offer both local delivery and nationwide shipping services. For local deliveries within 50 miles, we provide complimentary white-glove delivery service. For longer distances, we work with trusted transport companies that specialize in luxury and exotic vehicles. All shipments are fully insured and tracked throughout the delivery process.

How do I stay updated on new arrivals?
+

Join our VIP list to receive exclusive previews of new arrivals, special events, and member-only offers. You can sign up for our newsletter on our website, follow us on social media, or speak with one of our specialists about setting up personalized alerts for specific makes, models, or criteria that interest you.
