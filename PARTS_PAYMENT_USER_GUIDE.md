# Parts Payment System - User Guide

## How to Buy Parts from Inventory

### Step 1: Browse Parts Inventory
1. Navigate to **Inventory** section from the main menu
2. Browse through available car parts
3. View parts by categories: Engine Parts, Brake System, Suspension, etc.

### Step 2: View Part Details
1. Click on any part card to view detailed information
2. See specifications, features, and pricing
3. Check availability status (In Stock, Low Stock, Pre-Order, Out of Stock)

### Step 3: Purchase a Part
1. On the part detail page, click the **"⚡ Buy Now"** button
2. If not logged in, you'll be redirected to login page
3. After login, you'll be taken to the payment page

### Step 4: Complete Payment
1. **Review Part Summary**: Verify part details, brand, and price
2. **Select Quantity**: Use +/- buttons to adjust quantity (1-10 items)
3. **Review Total**: Total price updates automatically
4. **Fill Billing Information**:
   - Name (pre-filled from your profile)
   - Email (pre-filled)
   - Phone number (pre-filled if available)
   - Billing/Shipping address
5. **Select Payment Method**: Choose from Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Cash
6. Click **"🔒 Complete Purchase"** button

### Step 5: Order Confirmation
1. You'll see a success page with:
   - Order ID and Transaction ID
   - Complete order details
   - Part information and quantity
   - Total amount paid
   - Shipping information
2. Your order will be **"Processing"** initially

### Step 6: Track Your Orders
1. Go to **Dashboard** or **My Orders** page
2. View all your orders in two sections:
   - **Vehicle Orders**: Car purchases
   - **Parts Orders**: Part purchases
3. Check order status:
   - **Processing**: Order is being prepared (1-2 business days)
   - **Shipped**: Order has been dispatched (tracking number provided)
   - **Delivered**: Order successfully delivered

### Order Status Flow
```
Processing → Shipped → Delivered
   (1-2 days)  (3-5 days)  (Complete)
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I buy multiple quantities of the same part?**  
A: Yes! You can purchase 1-10 units of any part in a single order.

**Q: What if a part is out of stock?**  
A: You won't be able to complete the purchase. The system will alert you and redirect back to the part details page.

**Q: Can I cancel a parts order?**  
A: Currently, you need to contact support to cancel. Use the "📞 Contact Support" button on the order details.

**Q: How long does shipping take?**  
A: Orders are processed within 1-2 business days and typically shipped within 3-5 business days.

**Q: Will I receive a tracking number?**  
A: Yes! Once your order ships, a tracking number will be displayed in your order details.

**Q: Can I change my shipping address after ordering?**  
A: Contact support immediately using the support button in your order details.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**  
A: We accept Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, and Cash on Delivery.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**  
A: Yes! All transactions are processed through secure SSL encryption.

**Q: Can I view my past orders?**  
A: Yes! Go to "My Orders" in the dashboard to see all your vehicle and parts purchases.

**Q: What if I receive a damaged part?**  
A: Contact support immediately with order details and photos. We'll arrange a replacement.
